## 1. Character reference sheet

### C1: THE GIRL (age 9, Class 4)
- **Face:** oval face, soft round cheeks, warm dusky-brown skin, **large dark-brown almond eyes with an unusually steady, intense gaze**, thick straight eyebrows, small nose, full lips, a tiny dark mole under the **left** eye. She never smiles in the film.
- **Hair:** thick jet-black oiled hair, centre parting, **two tight plaits tied with red satin ribbons**, small loose baby hairs at the temples.
- **Outfit:** 1990s Kerala government-school uniform: **white short-sleeved cotton blouse with a rounded Peter-Pan collar, navy-blue pinafore skirt to the knees**, thin black leather sandals. Small **black bindi**, tiny gold stud earrings, one black thread bangle on the right wrist.
- **Body:** slim, small for her age, straight upright posture, hands usually still at her sides.
- **Signature expressions:** calm and unblinking; a faint lift of the chin; eyes slowly lifting toward the camera.

### C2: THE WOMAN (the same girl, grown up, ~24)
- **Face:** the **same** face as C1, matured: same almond eyes, same thick eyebrows, **same mole under the left eye**, sharper cheekbones, kohl-lined eyes, small black bindi.
- **Hair:** **waist-length, loose, thick jet-black hair**, slightly wavy, moving like a heavy curtain.
- **Outfit:** **cream-white Kerala kasavu set-saree with a broad gold zari border**, pallu falling over the left shoulder, cream blouse with gold edging. Barefoot, thin silver anklets (a soft chime when she walks). Single string of jasmine in the hair in Scenes 8 and 10 only.
- **Body:** tall, slender, unhurried; walks barefoot with a gliding, weightless step.
- **"Yakshi" state (Scenes 12 and 16 only):** same woman and costume, eyes catch a faint **amber glow**, hair lifts slightly as if underwater, a pale moon-rim light outlines her. She never turns grotesque. She stays beautiful and in control.

### C3: THE TEACHER (~45)
- **Face:** round face, wheat-brown skin, **thick round black-framed glasses**, a red sindoor line in the parting, kind laugh lines she tries to hide.
- **Hair:** greying black hair in a low bun with a few loose strands.
- **Outfit:** **mustard-yellow cotton saree with a maroon border**, matching maroon blouse, steel wristwatch on the left wrist, chalk dust on her fingers.
- **Prop:** a thin **rattan cane (chooral)**, about 60 cm, light brown with a worn grip.

### C4: CLASSMATES (8–12 kids, age 8–10)
- Same uniform as C1 (white shirts, navy shorts or pinafores). A mix of hairstyles, oiled side-parted hair for the boys. Exaggerated open-mouth laughter, hands slapping wooden desks.
- **Two named background kids** for continuity: **Boy A** (front row, gap-toothed, very loud laugh, red pen behind his ear) and **Girl B** (second row, puff-sleeves, covers her mouth when laughing).

### C5: "THE MEN" (symbolic only)
- **Never** show their faces clearly: tall shadow silhouettes with glinting eyes, in wet mundus, half-dissolved into fog. Used only in Scenes 9 and 11.

### Locations
- **L1 Classroom:** old Kerala school, laterite-red lower walls with whitewashed upper walls, wooden window bars with monsoon-grey daylight, long wooden benches and desks carved with initials, black chalkboard with "IV-B" and the date, terracotta tiled roof with visible rafters, a ceiling fan turning slowly.
