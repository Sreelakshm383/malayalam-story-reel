# തെങ്ങിൻതൈ — The Coconut Sapling

Shadow Puppetry · vertical 1080×1920 · 24 fps · ~52 s · Malayalam dialogue, Malayalam + English subtitles

## Brief (as agreed)

- A Malayalam motivational reel; style Shadow Puppetry.
- **"Conversation fluency akkanam"**: dialogue in everyday spoken Malayalam (ഇതിപ്പോ, എടുക്കൂലേ, കിട്ടുവോ, നടുന്നേ), short replies, gaps of 0.2–0.4 s between speakers, no read-aloud pauses.
- **Levels: dialogue voice 100, background voice 75.** Character dialogue is the loudest element. The narrator ("background voice") sits at 75 % of the dialogue level (−2.5 dB). Music and foley sit below both and duck further under any voice.
- Voices: Microsoft neural (edge-tts) `ml-IN-SobhanaNeural` and `ml-IN-MidhunNeural`, cast by pitch and rate:
  Ammumma = Sobhana −20 Hz, −14 %; Appu (boy) = Sobhana +24 Hz, +8 %; Minnu (girl) = Sobhana +32 Hz, +8 %; old Appu = Midhun −14 Hz, −12 %; narrator = Midhun −4 Hz, −6…−10 %.
- Subtitles: Malayalam on top, English under it, on the storyteller's placard.
- Setting: the medium stays shadow theatre (STYLE.md), dressed for Kerala: mundu and neriyathu with a kasavu border, coconut palms, a brass nilavilakku as the stage lamp, chenda-like drum and ilathalam-like cymbals in the score.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **One conversation, then time.** Grandmother plants; a boy questions her; she answers; time passes in one locked shot; the boy, now old, is asked the same question by his granddaughter. *Echo ending, one turn, one native move at the peak.*
2. **A procession of planters.** One long lateral pan along a row of palms, each planted by a different generation, each carved in a different dye; the narrator counts generations. *Beautiful, but no dialogue — the user asked for conversation.*
3. **Backstage, two puppeteers.** An old and a young puppeteer's hands argue through their puppets about planting. *Clever, but it splits attention from the story and the dialogue.*

**Chosen: 1.** It is built on conversation, which the user asked for, and its ending repeats its opening line in a new mouth — the lesson lands without being preached, and the narrator only has to say it once.

## 2. Logline and arc

A boy laughs at his grandmother for planting a coconut tree she will never harvest; years later, as an old man, he plants one for his granddaughter.

- **Setup:** dawn, one lamp. Ammumma kneels and plants a sapling. Appu runs in with a tender coconut.
- **Turn:** "This tender coconut you drank came from a tree my grandma planted." The camera tilts up the old palm behind them.
- **Ending:** the sapling grows into a tall palm in one locked shot while Ammumma lifts off the screen and Appu ages; under that palm, his granddaughter asks his own question. He answers. Pull back into the dark room; title plaque; the lamp is carried away.

## 3. Benchmark

- *Learn from* Lotte Reiniger's profile acting (one clear gesture per line, empty space between figures) and Kerala's Tholpavakoothu performances (one oil-lamp row, drum-led phrasing, figures pressed flat to the cloth).
  Learn also from short vertical "moral story" reels: hook in 2 s, one line on screen at a time, ending line held long enough to screenshot.
- *Don't take:* any historical puppet design, any recorded melody or drum pattern, the demo's Hou Yi story, suns, archer or opening.

## 6. Cue map

- Tempo 96 bpm (beat 0.625 s) where there is pulse.
- **Intro (0–6.4):** match, lamp flares, a single low drum at 2.6 + cymbal; then a soft bamboo flute phrase (pentatonic, huan-yin) under n1.
- **Conversation (6.4–16.0):** sparse: pellet drum and woodblock on Appu's run and freeze; a fiddle-like drone very low; each speaker change gets nothing — the voices carry it.
- **Near-silence (16.0–16.8):** bed cut, one woodblock tap.
- **The answer (16.8–26.8):** flute alone, slow, ku-yin colour (lowered 7th) under g2, resolving to huan-yin on g3.
- **Years (26.8–35.6):** gong scrape in; barrel drum on each swap beat (28.0, 29.8, 31.6, 33.4) with an accelerating roll between; cymbals on lamp relights; big falling gong at 35.0.
- **Echo (35.6–41.4):** flute returns with the opening phrase (the motif) under m1/p1.
- **Ending (41.4–52):** low drum heartbeat under n3, small rising gong at the title plaque 45.4, hard stop at 51.6 with only the lamp sizzle tail.

## 8. Subtitle and title design

- **Storyteller's placard** at the bottom of the vertical frame, above platform UI safe area (bottom 300 px left clear): narrow dark-lacquer board, thin gold line, small carved seal. Malayalam line on top in Noto Serif Malayalam SemiBold ~50 px; English under it in Cormorant Garamond 600 ~40 px; cream on dark; short slide in; hold ≥ max(1.8 s, speech + 0.6 s).
- A speaker's name is never written; the voice and the active puppet's small head lift tell who speaks.
- **Title plaque** = a dark hide plaque on rods with "തെങ്ങിൻതൈ" cut out (glows with lamp light), and "The Coconut Sapling" carved smaller beneath; pressed on, held 4+ s.
